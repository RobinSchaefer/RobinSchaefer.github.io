About me

I am a Computational Linguist with a background in Psycholinguistics and Data Science. While my research interests have become increasingly computational during the years, my current interests mainly circle around two problems:

  • LLM Benchmarking: Working at Bundesdruckerei, my main focus lies on LLM benchmarking in the context of public administration. As part of an ongoing projekt, I contribute to MÖVE, a framework for assessing LLMs across different criteria, including both performance (summarization, QA, etc.) as well as as governance dimensions relevant for the public sector (transparency, sustainability, etc.).
  • Argumentation:As a PhD student in the Applied CL Discourse Research Lab headed by Manfred Stede, I work on the computational detection of argumentation structure in tweets. Specifically, I am focusing on English and German data using supervised and unsupervised machine learning methods.
